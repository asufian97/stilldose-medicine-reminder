StillDose • Stillwave Studio Last updated: 18 October 2026

Your information Medicines, schedules, dose history, doctors, appointments, notes, and the photos and prescriptions you choose are stored on this device. No account is required.

How information is used Your entries display your schedule, deliver local reminders, and maintain your personal records. The app does not send these records to our servers. It contains no advertising or analytics trackers.

Permissions Notifications and alarm access support reminders. Camera access is requested when you choose to take a photo. You select the photos and documents to import. Reminders are restored after a restart. You can change permissions in your device settings.

Opening other apps Opening a prescription in another app gives that app temporary read access to the selected file. Calling a doctor opens your phone dialer. Those apps handle information under their own privacy policies.

Retention and deletion Records remain on this device until deleted or the app is uninstalled. Use “Delete all local data” in Settings to erase local records. Medical records and private files are excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer. The app has no cloud sync or restore feature.

Privacy and limitations You can hide medicine details on the lock screen in Settings. Protect your phone with a screen lock. Battery and system settings may delay reminders. StillDose does not provide medical advice; follow your healthcare professional's instructions.

Contact and changes For support or privacy questions, email sufianwp97dot@gmail.com. This policy will be updated if our information practices change.
